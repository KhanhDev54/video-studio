VIDEO STUDIO - HUONG DAN NHANH

1. Chay VideoStudio.exe.
2. Can FFmpeg: cai bang lenh "winget install Gyan.FFmpeg" (mo lai may hoac cua so lenh sau khi cai),
   hoac giai nen ffmpeg.exe + ffprobe.exe vao thu muc "ffmpeg\bin" canh VideoStudio.exe.
3. (Khong bat buoc) Khop mieng chinh xac hon: tai Rhubarb Lip Sync ban Windows tai
   https://github.com/DanielSWolf/rhubarb-lip-sync/releases va giai nen vao thu muc "rhubarb"
   canh VideoStudio.exe.
4. Thu muc "examples" co nhan vat mau va dan nhan vat de thu che do Hoat hinh.
